# How to get past the "Black Hole" screen



## Common Fixes:

- Make sure your Fortnite Porting is updated to the most recent release. You can download the latest version of Fortnite Porting [here](https://github.com/h4lfheart/FortnitePorting/releases/latest)!
- Check your Fortnite Version is up to date. If you are on the most recent update of Fortnite, Verify your files in the Epic Games Launcher.
- Increase your "Request Timeout Length" to a larger value, like 240. (You can adjust your Timeout Length in the Developer section of the settings tab. Ensure "Show Dev Settings" is enabled first).
- If a new Fortnite update released recently, Mappings and AES Keys may not be available yet.

::: Warning
These fixes are not guaranteed to stop the black hole screen from appearing, these fixes are just the most common errors.
:::
